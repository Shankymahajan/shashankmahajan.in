The interface

The calendar

The main interface, the primary place to view and manage a schedule. Designed to be clean and effortless so users can quickly scan through their events.

01, Natural Language Processing

Just say what you need

Users interact with the calendar in a conversation to input event details, title, duration and attendees, with no forms to fight.

"Create a meeting with Dinesh on back-office review for 2 hours."
"Going out to meet the doctor. I'll be out for 2 hours."
"Create a meeting with Abir and Aayush for 1 hour on design-system components."
"I'll be on a day off."

Comprehensive task builder

Add a task, honestly

An intuitive chat-style form gathers just enough detail, then does the hard part: checking whether a new task even fits. When it doesn't, it says so, and offers to move the deadline or reprioritise.

The moment it earns trust

"It'll take me 12 hours and the deadline is 31 May."

"Oh no, based on your schedule you won't finish by May 31st, you have other tasks. Can we move the deadline? 31 May → 3 June", with Prioritise this task or Update it.

02, Large Language Models

Event details that think ahead

Buffer-time optimisation

A sidebar to view and edit event info, plus insights drawn from team members' patterns, so scheduling accounts for reality, not the ideal.

"Abir usually joins 15 mins late. Don't worry, we'll add a buffer for this."

03, Actionable insights

The "Ahaan" moment
