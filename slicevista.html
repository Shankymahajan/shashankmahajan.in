01, What is SliceVista

Own a slice,
not the whole thing.

SliceVista is a real-estate marketplace where users buy and sell properties as fractional investments, co-owning premium commercial real estate for a fraction of the full ticket, with the paperwork and management handled for them.

02, The problem

Real estate is
locked behind a crore.

The traditional model restricted buyers to full property ownership, demanding significant capital and extensive paperwork. That left accessibility and affordability out of reach for most would-be investors. SliceVista's bet: break the asset into slices anyone can afford.

Discover, research

What was broken elsewhere

We started by examining direct competitors and their products. Using them myself surfaced a stack of very fixable problems:

📞 Spamming, excessive sales calls from the company.

🔒 Lack of trust, is my investment even secure?

🏦 No loan options inside the products.

🗂️ Disorganised info, poor hierarchy on the homepage.

🔕 No property updates after investing.

📱 Barely any mobile apps, only one competitor had one.

Mapping SliceVista against Strata Prop, Property Share and PropReturns showed clear gaps to own, starting from a ₹10,000 entry point, a real mobile app, and in-product loans.

Ideate, flows & IA

Mapping the journey

I mapped the path to a first investment, sign up → KYC → view property → connect with the asset manager → paperwork → done, then structured the whole platform around it.

Sign up / Login→ Complete KYC→ View property→ Connect with manager→ Investment done

UI design, moodboard

Clean, minimal, trustworthy

My primary inspiration came from modern architecture, fitting for the category. I aimed for a design that exudes cleanliness and minimalism, instilling confidence and trust in a brand new to the market.

UI design, key screens

The product, screen by screen

Value before sign-up

We wanted to give value before asking for KYC. The new-user home leads with featured, currently-open properties and gently nudges toward sign-up, motivating people to browse first.

Trust from day one

Once logged in (KYC pending), we always show the assigned asset manager, a real person behind the account to build trust while buying real estate, and nudge the user to complete KYC.

A portfolio at a glance

For existing investors: a clear picture of the portfolio and ROI. Investment KPIs up top, a notice board for updates (rent received, investment status, holiday bookings), and a direct line to the asset manager.

The property detail page

Where the decision happens. I followed a Z-pattern toward the CTA, kept the Invest button green (reserved only for investing) with dark-blue secondaries, added sticky tabs for the many property parameters, and surfaced our loan USP and the builder's profile.

Invest is sticky on scroll, the decision is always one tap away.

Investing, transparently

When a user shows interest we call and walk them through it, then the manager continues offline. We ask their preferred time slot (so we don't over-call) and show a live status tracker, interest shown, loan applied, token received, loan approved, so the whole process stays transparent.

My properties

Where users track investments: stats and ROI of each property, rental income up front, and, because these are real assets, the ability to book a holiday home and stay at their own property.
