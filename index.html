02, The story so far

I make software
feel less like
homework.

I started out drawing UI mockups in a notebook because I couldn't afford the software. Turns out constraints are a superpower. Today I lead design on products used by millions, but I still sketch first, argue with engineers second, and ship things I'm proud of third.

I care about the boring-but-critical stuff (accessibility, edge cases, loading states) as much as the flashy hero moments. Design isn't decoration, it's the argument, made visible.
